Leading role 01 · 2026

Blasco
OS

Several hardware programs on the same board, controlled without a single cable.

  • CoresFreeRTOS: Core 0 handles networking, storage and OTA; Core 1 is dedicated to the hardware.
  • AccessTelnet, WebSockets and Cloudflare Tunnel with HMAC-SHA256: from the internet, with no public IP.
  • Blasco AIA Gemini-powered assistant that receives live telemetry with every message.
  • HardwareNFC, DHT11 and HC-SR04 with auto-recovery and pins you can remap from the captive portal.
  • Running2,000+ hours of uptime without issues and 2,000+ rows in its database.
