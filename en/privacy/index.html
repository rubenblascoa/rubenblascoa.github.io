Privacy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

  • No cookiesNeither first- nor third-party. That’s why there’s no banner.
  • No formsNo accounts, sign-ups or fields to fill in.
  • No trackingOnly aggregated, anonymous visit statistics.

Who is responsible

This is a personal, non-commercial portfolio. The person responsible is Rubén Blasco Armengod. For any privacy question, email me at rubenblascoarmengod@gmail.com.

What is stored in your browser

Just one thing: if you pick a language with the ES / EN button, your browser saves that preference (localStorage, key lang) so the site opens in that language next time. It never leaves your browser, it isn’t sent anywhere and you can delete it at any time from your browser settings.

Visit statistics

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to know how many visits the site gets and which parts are seen most. According to Cloudflare, it works without cookies or browser storage and doesn’t follow you across websites. I only see aggregated data: pages viewed, country, browser and device type, where visits come from and load times. I never know who you are.

Services involved in loading the site

Like any website, when you open it your browser connects to the servers that deliver it, and they receive technical data such as your IP address and browser type. Everything (pages, fonts, images and libraries) is served from the site itself, so only two services are involved:

  • GitHub PagesHosts the site. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. GitHub privacy statement
  • CloudflareThe analytics described above. Cloudflare privacy policy

Links to other websites (GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, Blasco OS, FondTracker…) lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

If you email me

If you send me an email, I use your address and whatever you tell me only to reply. I don’t share it with anyone and you can ask me to delete it at any time.

Why and for how long

Statistics and technical data are processed on the basis of legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): keeping the site working and secure, and knowing anonymously whether it gets visited. Emails, because you choose to write to me. Emails are kept for as long as the conversation lasts; technical data, for as long as each service’s policy states.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by emailing the address above. If you believe your rights haven’t been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Changes

If what the site does with data changes, I’ll update this page and the date above.
